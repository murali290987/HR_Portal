# HR FAQ - General Employee Queries

## Leave Policy

**Q: How many casual leaves am I entitled to per year?**
A: All full-time employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves (CL) per calendar year, credited at 1 per month.

**Q: How many sick leaves can I take?**
A: Employees get 10 sick leaves (SL) per year. Sick leaves exceeding 2 consecutive days require a medical certificate.

**Q: Can I carry forward unused leaves to next year?**
A: Casual leaves cannot be carried forward. Earned leaves (EL) can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.

**Q: How do I apply for leave?**
A: Leave requests must be raised through the HRMS portal at least 2 working days in advance, except for sick leave.

## Payroll & Benefits

**Q: When is salary credited?**
A: Salary is credited on the last working day of every month.

**Q: How do I download my payslip?**
A: Payslips are available under HRMS > Payroll > Payslips, typically by the 3rd working day of the following month.

**Q: What is the notice period for resignation?**
A: The standard notice period is 60 days for employees at Associate level and above, and 30 days for Trainees/Interns.

**Q: How do I claim medical insurance?**
A: Raise a claim via the insurance partner portal (linked in HRMS > Benefits) within 30 days of the medical event, along with original bills.

## Work From Home / Hybrid Policy

**Q: How many WFH days am I allowed per month?**
A: Employees may work from home up to 8 days per month, subject to manager approval, except for roles marked "Office Mandatory."

**Q: Do I need to inform HR separately for WFH?**
A: No, WFH requests only need manager approval via HRMS > Attendance > WFH Request. HR does not need to be copied.

## Appraisal & Promotion

**Q: When does the appraisal cycle happen?**
A: The annual appraisal cycle runs from April to June, with revised compensation effective July 1st.

**Q: How can I access my appraisal letter?**
A: Appraisal letters are published under HRMS > My Documents > Appraisal Letters after the cycle is closed and approved.

**Q: Can I raise a dispute on my appraisal rating?**
A: Yes, disputes can be raised within 7 days of rating disclosure via HRMS > Appraisal > Raise Concern, routed to your reporting manager and HRBP.
