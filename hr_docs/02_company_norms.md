# Company Norms & Code of Conduct

## Attendance & Working Hours

1. Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.
2. Employees are expected to log attendance via the HRMS biometric/mobile app at the start and end of each working day.
3. A grace period of 15 minutes is allowed for late login, up to 3 times per month.
4. Habitual late login (more than 3 times a month, for 3 consecutive months) will trigger an HR review.

## Dress Code

1. Business casuals are mandatory Monday to Thursday.
2. Casual wear is permitted on Fridays, excluding client-facing roles.
3. Employees attending client meetings must adhere to formal business attire regardless of the day.

## Code of Conduct

1. Employees must not share confidential company data, client information, or source code with any external party without written authorization.
2. Any conflict of interest (e.g., a close relative working in a vendor or competitor organization) must be disclosed to HR within 15 days of joining or occurrence.
3. Harassment of any kind — verbal, physical, or online — is strictly prohibited and subject to disciplinary action under the company's POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) policy.
4. Employees found misusing company assets (laptops, access cards, software licenses) for personal commercial gain will be subject to disciplinary action, including termination.

## Travel & Expense Norms

1. Domestic travel must be booked through the approved travel desk at least 5 working days in advance.
2. Per diem for domestic travel is INR 1500/day for Associate level, INR 2000/day for Manager and above.
3. Expense claims must be submitted within 15 days of travel completion, with original bills/e-receipts attached.
4. Expenses above INR 10,000 require pre-approval from the reporting manager and finance controller.

## Exit Process

1. Employees resigning must serve the notice period as defined in their offer letter (30 or 60 days).
2. A no-dues clearance must be completed across IT, Admin, Finance, and HR before the full and final settlement is processed.
3. Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
4. Relieving letter and experience certificate are issued only after successful no-dues clearance.
