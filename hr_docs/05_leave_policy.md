# Leave Policy — Detailed

## Types of Leave

### 1. Casual Leave (CL)
- 12 days per calendar year, credited monthly (1/month).
- Maximum 3 consecutive CLs can be taken at a stretch without manager escalation.
- Cannot be carried forward or encashed.

### 2. Sick Leave (SL)
- 10 days per calendar year.
- Medical certificate mandatory for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- Unused sick leave lapses at year-end; not encashable.

### 3. Earned Leave (EL) / Privilege Leave
- Accrued at 1.5 days per month (18 days/year) after completion of 1 year of service.
- Can be carried forward up to 30 days; beyond that, auto-encashed at year-end.
- Requires minimum 5 working days advance notice.

### 4. Maternity Leave
- 26 weeks of paid leave as per the Maternity Benefit Act, applicable for the first two children.
- Can be availed up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

### 5. Paternity Leave
- 15 calendar days of paid leave, to be availed within 3 months of childbirth.

### 6. Bereavement Leave
- 5 days of paid leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member.

### 7. Loss of Pay (LOP)
- Applicable when an employee has exhausted all applicable leave balances.
- LOP days are deducted proportionately from the monthly gross salary.

## Approval Workflow

1. Employee raises a leave request in HRMS with reason and dates.
2. Request routes to the direct reporting manager for approval.
3. For leaves exceeding 5 consecutive working days, HRBP approval is also required.
4. Sick leave for illness can be applied post-facto (within 3 working days of returning).

## Public Holidays

- The company observes 10 fixed public holidays per year, published in HRMS > Calendar at the start of the calendar year.
- Employees in client-facing roles may be asked to work on a public holiday with compensatory off provided within 30 days.
