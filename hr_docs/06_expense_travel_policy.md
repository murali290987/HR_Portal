# Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Domestic Travel

| Grade | Air Travel | Hotel (per night cap) | Per Diem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trainee/Associate | Economy | INR 4,000 | INR 1,500 |
| Senior Associate/Manager | Economy (flexible) | INR 6,000 | INR 2,000 |
| Senior Manager & above | Business (sectors > 3 hrs) | INR 9,000 | INR 2,500 |

## International Travel

- All international travel requires VP-level approval, submitted at least 15 working days in advance.
- Forex advance can be claimed up to 80% of estimated per diem, adjusted post-trip.
- Travel insurance is mandatory and arranged by the Admin team.

## Expense Claim Process

1. Log the claim in the Expense Portal within 15 days of expense incurred.
2. Attach original bills/e-invoices; claims without valid bills above INR 500 will be rejected.
3. Claims are auto-routed to the reporting manager, then to Finance for disbursement.
4. Reimbursement is processed within 10 working days of manager approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

- Alcohol and personal entertainment expenses.
- Traffic fines or penalties incurred during official travel.
- Personal phone/data charges beyond the monthly official allowance cap (INR 1,000/month).

## Local Conveyance

- Employees using personal vehicles for official work can claim INR 8/km (two-wheeler) or INR 12/km (four-wheeler), capped at INR 2,000/month.
- Cab claims for late-night work (post 9 PM) are reimbursed at actuals, subject to manager approval, up to INR 500/trip.
