# Exit & Relieving Process

## Resignation

1. Employee submits resignation via HRMS > Exit > Initiate Resignation, specifying the last working day.
2. Notice period is as per the offer letter: 30 days (Trainee/Associate) or 60 days (Senior Associate and above).
3. Manager and HRBP are notified automatically upon submission.

## Notice Period Buyout

- Employees may request to buy out the remaining notice period (subject to business approval), paid at the employee's last drawn gross salary rate for the remaining days.
- Buyout requests must be raised at least 15 days before the intended last working day.

## Knowledge Transfer (KT)

- A KT plan must be documented and signed off by the reporting manager before the last working day.
- Incomplete KT may result in delayed full and final settlement processing.

## No-Dues Clearance

Clearance is required across the following departments before relieving:
1. **IT** — laptop, access card, software license deactivation.
2. **Admin** — parking pass, ID card, physical assets.
3. **Finance** — outstanding advances, loan recoveries, expense settlements.
4. **HR** — leave encashment calculation, exit interview completion.

## Full & Final (F&F) Settlement

- Processed within 45 days of the last working day.
- Includes: pro-rated salary, encashment of unused Earned Leave, deductions for notice period shortfall (if any), and recovery of any pending dues.

## Documents Issued Post-Exit

- Relieving Letter — issued after successful no-dues clearance.
- Experience Certificate — issued along with the relieving letter.
- Form 16 — issued by the following April, covering the period of employment in that financial year.

## Rehire Eligibility

- Employees who resign with proper notice and a "Recommended" exit rating are eligible for rehire after a minimum gap of 6 months.
- Employees terminated for disciplinary reasons are marked "Not Eligible for Rehire" in the HR system.
