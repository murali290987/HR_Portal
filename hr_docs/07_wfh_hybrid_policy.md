# Work From Home & Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility

- All employees classified as "Hybrid Eligible" in HRMS can avail of Work From Home (WFH).
- Roles marked "Office Mandatory" (e.g., Facilities, Front Desk, certain client-onsite roles) are not eligible.

## WFH Allowance

- Up to 8 WFH days per calendar month, non-carry-forward.
- WFH beyond 8 days in a month requires Skip-Level Manager approval, capped at 12 days/month.
- Extended WFH (more than 5 consecutive working days) requires HRBP notification.

## Process

1. Raise a WFH request under HRMS > Attendance > WFH Request at least 1 day in advance (same-day allowed for emergencies with manager approval).
2. Manager approval is auto-escalated if not actioned within 24 hours.
3. Employees must remain reachable during core hours (11 AM – 4 PM) while on WFH.

## Equipment & Reimbursement

- Employees working remotely full-time (Remote-first roles) are eligible for a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR 15,000.
- Internet reimbursement of INR 750/month is provided to Remote-first and Hybrid employees, claimed quarterly.

## Client-Facing Considerations

- Employees on active client engagements must align WFH days with client's remote-work policy, which may override the standard company policy.
